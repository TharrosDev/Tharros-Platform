# Membership & Pricing

## Membership tiers

We offer three annual membership tiers:

- **Basic — $0/year:** standard pricing, access to seasonal sales.
- **Plus — $49/year:** 10% off every order and free standard shipping with no minimum.
- **Premier — $99/year:** 15% off every order, free express shipping, and early access to new collections.

## Price matching

We match the advertised price of any identical, in-stock item sold by a major Canadian retailer. Submit a price-match request within **7 days** of your purchase and we will refund the difference.

## Gift cards

Gift cards are available in denominations of $25, $50, and $100. Gift cards never expire and cannot be redeemed for cash.
