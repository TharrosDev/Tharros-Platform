# Shipping Policy

## Domestic shipping

We ship across Canada. **Standard shipping is free on all orders over $75.** For orders under $75, standard shipping is a flat **$8.95**.

## Delivery estimates

- **Standard:** 3 to 5 business days
- **Express:** 1 to 2 business days for an additional **$19.95**

Orders placed before **1:00 PM ET** on a business day ship the same day. Orders placed after the cutoff, or on weekends, ship the next business day.

## International shipping

We ship to the United States. International orders typically arrive within **7 to 12 business days**. Customs duties and import taxes are the responsibility of the customer.

## Tracking

A tracking number is emailed automatically once your order leaves our warehouse.
