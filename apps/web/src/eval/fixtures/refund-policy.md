# Refund & Returns Policy

## Returns window

Customers may return any unopened item within **30 days** of the delivery date for a full refund. Opened items may be returned within **14 days** for store credit only.

## Condition requirements

Returned products must be in their original packaging with all tags attached. Items marked "Final Sale" cannot be returned or exchanged under any circumstances.

## How refunds are issued

Approved refunds are credited to the original payment method within **5 to 7 business days** after we receive the returned item. Original shipping charges are non-refundable unless the return is the result of our error.

## Restocking fee

A **15% restocking fee** applies to returns of large furniture items. No restocking fee applies to clothing, accessories, or small goods.

## Damaged or defective items

If an item arrives damaged or defective, contact support within **48 hours** of delivery and we will arrange a free replacement or full refund, including shipping.
